How I Write With AI

By Pedro Santos · November 2026 · 2 min read

I use AI heavily to write what I publish here. It drafts, it edits, it tightens, it polishes, it checks my sources and argues with my structure. Most sentences on this site went through an AI model at least once, and many started there.

Two things never come from the model.

  • The ideas are 100% mine. The arguments, the positions, the experience they come from, and the decision about what is worth saying. The model does not get to decide what I think.
  • The responsibility is 100% mine. I read every piece before it ships, I check every claim and every number against its source, and if something here is wrong, it is my mistake, not the tool's.

Why I am unapologetic about it

Most of what I write is about teams letting AI agents write their code, safely. If AI is good enough to write the code I put my name to, it is good enough to help me write about it. Hiding it would be the strange choice.

The standard is the same in both cases. The tool can produce as much as it likes. What makes the result trustworthy is a person who decides what it should say, checks what it does say, and answers for it afterwards. That is the whole argument of Pedro's Law, applied to prose: many prompts to shape it, and my approval before it ships.

If you find a mistake, tell me. It is mine to fix.
